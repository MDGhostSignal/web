## The Steward · C-P-C

**Live tagline:** The Guardian of Sacred Fire.

### H1

You belong with people who protect what is good and take their time making it.

### H2

You belong with people who keep the fire, and who value the time the making takes.

### H3

The time is part of the work. Keeping the fire is how that time is shared. Someone else can stand in the warmth, and it is still the same fire.

### H4

You are looking for the partner who stands on the same values you do. The trust between you is not a balance you arrive with. It is prepared, because the ground underneath you already matches. The vow is the same for a brand or a creator: keep the fire, and give the process the time it takes to share it with the people who belong beside it.

### Paragraph

The Steward keeps the fire and values the time the process takes. That time is how the work stays good, and how its warmth is shared with the person it is for. There is a place beside the fire for them. If you work this way, you will know the other one when you meet them.

### Long

You belong with people who protect what is good and take their time making it. The good thing is already here. It may be a name people trust, or a voice an audience lets into the week. It does not need a new costume. It needs a partner who will keep the fire lit and give the process the time it is worth.

A Steward stays close. They ask who the work is for, and they make room beside the fire for that person. What they share with the right partner is the ground under the work, the same values, not a trust already banked between them. Keeping the fire is how they share it, and the time it takes is part of what they are sharing. The people who belong there can tell it is still the same one.

Bring the brand or bring the show. The vow is the same. You are looking for someone who keeps the fire and values the time that sharing takes. When you find them, the partnership is a place you both belong.

## The Shepherd · C-P-L

**Live tagline:** The Community Unifier.

### H1

You belong with people who want more people included, without weakening what they already have.

### H2

You belong with people who can open the circle wider and still keep the welcome warm.

### H3

More people may come in. The room should still know why it gathered.

### H4

Growth, here, is hospitality with a longer reach. You want the partner who can carry a trusted circle into public and not lose the song it was singing.

### Paragraph

The Shepherd carries what is good to more people. The roots stay: human, steady, unwilling to water the principle down. What changes is the size of the welcome. A larger crowd that no longer knows itself is not the win. A larger circle that still does is. You are looking for the brand or the creator who can widen the door and keep the room kind.

### Long

You belong with people who want more people included, without weakening what they already have. There is already a circle. Listeners. Customers. A few people who know why they came. The work is to invite more of them in without turning the gathering into a crowd.

A Shepherd believes a welcome can travel. The reason for gathering stays visible. New people arrive as guests. You will know this partner because the bigger version still feels like the smaller one. The tone holds. The promise holds. The door is simply open farther.

If you hold the brand, you want a voice that can introduce you and have it feel like a welcome. If you hold the show, you want a brand that is glad to meet your people and unwilling to change what they already love. Together the circle goes public, and it stays warm.

## The Conservator · C-S-C

**Live tagline:** The Master Craftsman of Order.

### H1

You belong with people who care that the work is done right, and that it stays that way.

### H2

You belong with people who still recognize the work after other hands have touched it.

### H3

The standard is not paperwork around the piece. The standard is the piece. When it is finished, it should still be in tune.

### H4

Done right is the first half. Still right, once more than one person is making it, is the rest. You want a partner who treats that fidelity as the product.

### Paragraph

The Conservator keeps excellence from depending on one careful person in the room. Order, here, is a kindness. It is how the work survives contact with other hands and still comes out exact. If you only relax when the other person is as exact as you are, this is the partner you have been listening for.

### Long

You belong with people who care that the work is done right, and that it stays that way. Care is not a mood. It is a standard you can hand to someone else and still know the work when it returns.

You hear it in the edit, the claim, the line a show will not cross. A Conservator builds the habits that keep that standard standing when the schedule tightens and the room gets crowded. More hands may touch the work. The work does not go out of tune.

Bring the brand or bring the show. You are looking for the one who will hold the standard when you are not in the room. When both of you hear a false note in the same place, you belong together.

## The Institution Builder · C-S-L

**Live tagline:** The Legacy Architect.

### H1

You belong with people who want to build something that lasts longer than one project.

### H2

You belong with people who are willing to keep building after the first campaign has gone quiet.

### H3

A single good flight can open the door. The point is the house that is still standing when the flight is over.

### H4

You want a partner ready to found something with you. A point of view becomes a place other people can find, return to, and build on.

### Paragraph

The Institution Builder thinks past the current project. The horizon is years. The draw is something durable: a company, a show, a network, a practice that later work can stand on. One good collaboration is a beginning. The thing that remains is the partnership they came to make.

### Long

You belong with people who want to build something that lasts longer than one project. The project matters. It is how the door opens. The ambition is a structure that still holds the meaning after the project is done.

An Institution Builder asks what will still be here next year. A name people return to. A show others can join. A way of working that does not have to be invented again. Reach is welcome when the structure can carry it. Scale is how the meaning stays available, not how it gets spent.

You may be building the company, or you may be building the body of work. Either way, you are looking for someone who will stay long enough to become part of the place. If you are tired of work that ends when the invoice is paid, you belong with them.

## The Artisan Reformer · X-P-C

**Live tagline:** The Subversive Creator.

### H1

You belong with people who change things by making them better and more personal.

### H2

You belong with people who set a better, more human thing beside the old one, and let people feel the difference.

### H3

The change is not a speech. It is something a person can pick up. The older form can stay in the room. It has a neighbor now.

### H4

You want a partner who will change what you offer together without losing the hand on it. Better, and still somebody's.

### Paragraph

The Artisan Reformer changes the work by making it. The new version stays intimate enough to hold. People choose it because they can feel it, not because they were argued toward it. If you trust a made thing more than a manifesto, this is the partner whose table you want.

### Long

You belong with people who change things by making them better and more personal. A listener can hear it. A customer can feel it. The improvement is specific, close to a human hand, and more like the person it is for than what stood there before.

The old version does not have to be mocked. It simply meets a neighbor people would rather live with. That is the whole argument. Beauty and care do the persuading, quietly, and the room shifts.

Bring the offer or bring the voice. You are looking for someone who will make the work more personal, not merely louder. Together you replace a tired form with one people can feel. If that is how you believe a thing changes, you belong with them.

## The Catalyst · X-P-L

**Live tagline:** The Cultural Spark.

### H1

You belong with people who can get others to care, and to move with them.

### H2

You belong with people who can turn a private conviction into a fire a whole group will stand near.

### H3

Care without movement stays in the room. Movement without care is only noise. This partner holds both, while the feeling is still alive.

### H4

You want someone who can put a better way in front of a tired pattern and invite others onto it before the reason goes cold.

### Paragraph

The Catalyst wants change that reaches people. They can see what has worn out, set a better way of living beside it, and bring a group along while the reason is still warm. The people are the point. The size of the group matters only if the reason survives the trip. If you need a partner who can make others care and then actually go, you are close.

### Long

You belong with people who can get others to care, and to move with them. A true story that never leaves the room is unfinished. A burst of attention with nothing underneath it is unfinished too.

You will know this partner by what happens to other people. They do not only nod. They see themselves in the idea, and they move while the feeling is still living. It has the heat of a spark and the aim of a shared direction. Attention, here, is not the prize. Company on the way is.

If you hold the brand, you want a voice whose people can care about what you care about. If you hold the show, you want a brand with a reason your listeners can feel. Together you start the same fire in the same people.

## The Designer · X-S-C

**Live tagline:** The Strategic Innovator.

### H1

You belong with people who fix a broken way of working by redesigning it.

### H2

You belong with people who will rebuild how the work is made, not only how it looks once it is finished.

### H3

A new coat of words leaves the break where it was. This partner draws the better form first, until it holds in the detail.

### H4

You want someone who retires a tired way of working only after a sturdier one is already standing. The second week should still make sense.

### Paragraph

The Designer reimagines the work through form, care, and intention. The blueprint comes before the announcement. A broken brief, a clumsy handoff, a partnership that only works when everyone is excited: these are joints, and this partner can hear them fail. If you would rather redraw the thing than decorate it, you belong with them.

### Long

You belong with people who fix a broken way of working by redesigning it. The break may be the offer, the way a message enters a show, or the path two teams use to hand work across. Fresh language on top of that break does not heal it.

A Designer wants the replacement drawn before the old pattern is set down. You will know them by their care for the second week, when the novelty has cooled and another person has to follow the work and get the same result. Precision is how they are kind. The form is how other people can trust it.

Bring the company or bring the craft. You are looking for a partner who will change the structure with you, not ask you to perform your way around it. Together you leave a better way standing where the broken one was.

## The Architect · X-S-L

**Live tagline:** The Systemic Disruptor.

### H1

You belong with the people building a world that has room for you in it.

### H2

You belong with people laying out a world large enough that you can actually live and work inside it.

### H3

The world already standing stays. Beside it, a further one is being built, and it has a chair with your name on it.

### H4

"You" is particular. Your company. Your show. Your way of making a living. The right partner builds at the scale of a field, and does not ask you to disappear in order to enter.

### Paragraph

The Architect builds at the scale of a field: a market, a culture, a network. The work looks for the place where a new structure gives people room they did not have. Nothing here erases the world already built. A further world is added, so the people who were missing a place can find one with their work intact.

### Long

You belong with the people building a world that has room for you in it. "You" is not a crowd. It is a particular maker, a particular brand, a way of working the current plan did not leave a chair for. The Architect's work is to build the room where that chair exists.

The scale is larger than one campaign or one season. It may be a market others come to work in, a network other shows can join, a set of terms that make this kind of partnership ordinary. The world already standing is not the enemy. It remains in view. The new world is added so the right people can take part without handing over what makes them themselves.

You will know the test when you see the work. You can find yourself in it. If you have been looking for someone who builds at that scale and still saves you a place, you belong with them.
